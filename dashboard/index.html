What is each brand claiming?

Rows are brands, columns are claim territories. Each cell is how many of that brand’s live ads make that claim — click any cell to read the actual ads.

Who over-indexes where?

Index = the brand’s share of its own ads in a territory, divided by the category’s share. 2.0 means the brand is twice as focused there as the category average.

Which audiences is each claim aimed at?

Only counts ads whose copy explicitly signals an audience. Empty rows mean nobody is speaking to that group in search.

Which proof points recur?

How often each kind of evidence appears, and how many brands lean on it. A proof type used by one brand is a differentiator; one used by ten is table stakes.

Where is nobody playing?

Opportunity score = 100 × (0.6 × share of brands absent + 0.4 × share of ad volume missing vs. the busiest territory). Higher means emptier.
